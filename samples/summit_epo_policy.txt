SUMMIT EPO — SCHEDULE OF BENEFITS (SAMPLE)
Plan ID: SUM-EPO-2024-A
Network: Summit Preferred EPO (no out-of-network except emergencies)

COST SHARING
- Annual Deductible (Individual): $2,000
- Annual Deductible (Family): $4,000
- Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Individual): $7,500
- Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Family): $15,000
- Primary Care Visit: $35 copay after deductible
- Specialist Visit: $70 copay after deductible; referral required
- Emergency Room: $400 copay (waived if admitted within 24 hours)
- Urgent Care: $90 copay

IMAGING & DIAGNOSTICS
- X-ray: $40 copay after deductible
- MRI / CT: Prior authorization required; $200 copay after deductible
- Lab work: $0 after deductible at contracted labs

REHABILITATION
- Physical therapy: $50 copay per visit
- Prior authorization required after 12 visits per plan year
- Occupational therapy: $50 copay per visit

PHARMACY BENEFITS
- Tier 1 Generic: $15 copay
- Tier 2 Preferred Brand: $45 copay
- Tier 3 Non-Preferred: $90 copay
- Specialty drugs: 30% coinsurance after deductible; prior authorization required

EXCLUSIONS — NOT COVERED
- Cosmetic surgery and related complications
- Experimental or investigational treatments
- Infertility diagnosis and treatment
- Acupuncture and chiropractic care
- Dental implants and orthodontia
- Care outside the United States except true medical emergencies

PREVENTIVE CARE
- Annual wellness visit: $0 (deductible does not apply)
- ACA-recommended immunizations: $0
