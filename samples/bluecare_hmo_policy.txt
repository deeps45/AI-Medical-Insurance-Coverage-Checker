BLUECARE HMO — EVIDENCE OF COVERAGE (EXCERPT)
Member ID example plan: BLUE-HMO-100
Effective: Calendar year 2024

COST SHARING
- Annual Deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
- Out-of-Pocket Maximum: $4,000 individual / $8,000 family
- Primary Care Physician: $20 copay (deductible does not apply)
- Specialist: Requires referral; $40 copay
- Emergency Room: $150 copay
- Ambulance: $100 copay

DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES
- Lab work: $0 after deductible at in-network labs
- MRI: Prior authorization required; $75 copay after deductible
- X-ray: $30 copay

PHARMACY
- Generic: $5 copay
- Brand: $35 copay
- Mail-order 90-day generic: $12

PREVENTIVE CARE
- Annual wellness visit: $0
- Recommended immunizations: $0

NOT COVERED
- Acupuncture
- Infertility services
- Non-emergency out-of-network care without authorization
